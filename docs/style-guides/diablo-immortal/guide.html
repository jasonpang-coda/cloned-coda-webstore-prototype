Style Guide · v1.0 · 2026

Diablo Immortal

A free-to-play, fast-paced action RPG set between Diablo II and Diablo III — fight across a hell-scorched world as one of ten classes, solo or in a Warband.

Source: diabloimmortal.blizzard.com/en-us · © Blizzard Entertainment, Inc. · reference-derived fan style guide, not an official brand document.

01 · Brand Essence

Brutal · Gothic · Relentless · Epic

Diablo Immortal presents itself as an ancient, inscribed world under siege — a hell-scorched Sanctuary where the player is already powerful, not being coached into power. Every surface (type, colour, imagery) reinforces command and mythic scale over friendliness or convenience.

Brutal

CTA copy is imperative and violent in register — "Hell Obeys Me", "Diablo's Wrath Unleashed" — never soft or apologetic.

Gothic

The unicase, rune-marked Exocet Immortal display face and carved-stone dividers read as inscribed/engraved rather than typed.

Relentless

"Updates every two weeks, new challenges and rewards are always within reach" — the product frame is constant escalation, not a finished package.

Epic

"a massive campaign with over 30 hours of story" and "world-ending monsters" — scale is stated explicitly, not implied.

01·B · Brand Voice & Tone observed · live site

How Diablo Immortal Speaks

Grand and commanding, second-person imperative for calls to action, third person for lore — the copy never coaches the reader, it commands them.

Voice pillars

Imperative & commanding

Contrast & placement rules

  • White text on neutral #1A1715 ≈ 17.9:1 — always safe.
  • Primary #D7AB6D on neutral #1A1715 ≈ 8.5:1 — safe for body and large text.
  • White text on secondary #7B2917 CTA fill ≈ 9.7:1 — safe even at button-label sizes.
  • Never place gold text on the tertiary blue or on light art without a scrim — untested and likely below AA.
  • Status colours must always pair with a non-colour cue (icon, +/− sign, or label) — never colour alone.
04 · Token Architecture — 5-Tier Cascade

How the design system is structured

Every visual property resolves through this cascade. Tiers 1–3 are brand-locked. Tiers 4–5 are store-flexible defaults. The emitter.ts only injects ref seeds + optional palette ramps + font + page background — everything else resolves automatically. Figma token exports drop the --x- prefix; the repo adds it.

Type primitives + Ref seeds

2

Anchor rule: --x-palette-{role}-500 = var(--x-ref-{role})
The -500 step IS the ref seed. The ramp is built around it.
BRAND-LOCKED
3

--x-sys-colour-{role}-{subtle|soft|main|strong|heavy|inverse}

--x-sys-space-* · --x-sys-radius-* · --x-sys-stroke-{thin|medium|thick}

--x-bg-* · --x-text-* · --x-border-*

05 · Layout & Grid System System Constant

Non-Negotiable Layout

Identical in every guide. The brand never changes the grid. Responsiveness is driven by CSS @container queries against the device-screen container — never viewport @media. The screen sets container-type: inline-size; the overlay deliberately does not (it would trap position: fixed descendants).

07 · Component Style DNA

Visual Building Blocks

Shape language Brand-Locked

--x-sys-radius-s (~8px) on interactive pill/button chrome (observed on the live nav CTA); containers and thumbnails stay sharp (--x-sys-radius-0) — the softness is reserved for tappable controls only.

Lighting model Brand-Locked

Interactive: flat brick fill + baked highlight-texture image, not a CSS shadow. Non-interactive: flat dark panel behind painterly key art, no drop shadow.

Effect tints Brand-Locked

--x-sys-effect-shadow-primary: #141313; --x-sys-effect-glow-primary: #D7AB6D (ember); hover: #EAC98F; pressed: #5C4340.

Density & spacing Brand-Locked

--x-sys-space-l as base gap; rhythm is airy — large hero/section blocks with generous breathing room, consistent with a marketing surface.

Elevation Brand-Locked

Almost everything sits at --x-sys-elevation-flat; depth comes from painted/rendered art, not CSS shadow layering. Reserve level-2/3 for modal and toast only.

Stroke weight Brand-Locked

--x-sys-stroke-thin for any plain divider; the brand's real signature is image-based ornamental carved-stone dividers rather than a CSS border (see tier2SignatureSurfaces).

Material & texture Brand-Locked

Carved stone-relief, worn leather, and desert surface texture baked into imagery — never a CSS distress filter. No halftone or noise overlay observed.

Motion language Brand-Locked

[inferred] ~250ms ease-out; brightness/texture-swap feedback on CTA hover, consistent with the baked-texture button system.

Iconography Brand-Locked

Minimal — a plain line-style account icon was the only UI icon observed; classes are represented by full character renders, not icons.

Semantic colour mapping Store-Flexible

Default: bg-action-primary → sys-colour-secondary-main (brick, not gold — gold is reserved for text/labels). Stores may remap to any intensity step.

Interactive vs non-interactive rule

Interactive = elevation-flat + sys-colour-secondary-main fill + baked-texture highlight + states overlay; non-interactive = elevation-flat + painterly art background + no overlay, no shadow.

08 · Art Direction & VFX

Imagery System

Framing & subject

Character renders shown ¾ or full-body, weapon raised or mid-action, camera roughly eye-level to slightly low for heroic scale.

Background

Environmental hellscape or dark abstract VFX — never a flat solid colour behind a hero character.

Lighting & mood

Warm ember/fire key light from below or side, cool shadow fill, occasional cold arcane rim for magic-class characters.

VFX signature

Embers, smoke, arcane glow, blood-red highlights — tied to the featured class's theme (e.g. infernal fire for Warlock).

Figure-ground

Environmental depth rather than a clean cut-out — the character is composited into, not isolated from, the hellscape.

Text-over-art

A dark linear-gradient scrim (top illegible edge fading to ~66% black) sits behind all hero copy; wordmark and headline stay within the left/lower-left third.

✓ Do
  • Anchor headline copy over the darkest third of the composition
  • Let VFX (embers/glow) bleed past the character's silhouette into the type-safe zone sparingly
✗ Don't
  • Don't drop the scrim and place white type directly on bright art
  • Don't isolate characters on flat solid colour — it breaks the "composited into hell" read
