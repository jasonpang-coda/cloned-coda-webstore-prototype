01 · Brand Essence

Mythic · Combative · Electric

MCOC frames every visit as a return to an ongoing mythic war — Champions drawn from the whole of Marvel history clash in "The Contest," commanded by the player under the in-fiction rank of Summoner. The visual language backs this up: comic-realistic hero renders lit like key art, a single acid-green accent that reads as "alive/active," and gold-foil display type straight out of a collectible card border.

Mythic

Footer tagline states it outright: "THE GREATEST BATTLES IN MARVEL HISTORY ARE IN YOUR HANDS." Events are framed as sagas and acts, not features.

Combative

Hero art is exclusively dynamic action poses — gliders, bombs, blades mid-swing — never passive character-select stills.

Electric

A single acid-green (#ABFF00) glow-halo is the universal "this is active/selected/positive" signal against an otherwise near-black UI.

02 · Logo & Clear Space

Logo

Comic-style wordmark lockup, always reproduced on a near-black field per the reference site's nav bar. The word "CONTEST OF CHAMPIONS" sits beneath a bespoke "MARVEL" logotype treatment. Clear space: keep at least the cap-height of the "M" clear on every side; never crowd it against other UI.

✓ Approved colourways
  • Full-colour lockup on near-black (#0B0B0D–#141517)
  • Single-colour white on dark, for small/nav placements
✗ Misuse — never do this
  • Stretch or skew the lockup
  • Place on busy key art without a scrim

  • Add drop shadows or bevels not in the source

Contrast & placement rules

  • White on neutral bg (#141517): 18.27:1 — default body text
  • Primary lime on neutral bg: 14.82:1 — safe for large glow/label text
  • Secondary gold on neutral bg: 10.61:1 — safe for text and links
  • Tertiary magenta on neutral bg: 5.17:1 — passes AA body text; reserve for accents, not dense paragraphs
  • Caution orange on neutral bg: 8.71:1 · Negative red on neutral bg: 6.10:1 — both safe for text
  • Deep decorative red #BA0000 only reaches 2.69:1 — keep it to borders/gradient stops, never text
  • Status is never colour-only: the source UI pairs every status fill with an explicit label or icon
04 · Token Architecture — 5-Tier Cascade

How the design system is structured

Every visual property resolves through this cascade. Tiers 1–3 are brand-locked. Tiers 4–5 are store-flexible defaults. The emitter.ts only injects ref seeds + optional palette ramps + font + page background — everything else resolves automatically. Figma token exports drop the --x- prefix; the repo adds it.

07 · Component Style DNA

Visual Building Blocks

Shape language Brand-Locked

--x-sys-radius-xs for containers/thumbnails (observed 8px tile rounding); --x-sys-radius-full for pills/badges/avatars (observed 50%/60px circles); sharp 0px on structural dividers. Signature motif: angled, cut-corner octagonal HUD panel frames (SVG-clipped) around hub/accordion sections.

Lighting model Brand-Locked

Interactive = elevation-level-1 + acid-green glow halo (box-shadow 0 0 4px #ABFF00, 0 0 33px rgba(171,255,0,.3)). Non-interactive = elevation-flat, near-black fill, thin #333540 border, no glow.

Effect tints Brand-Locked

--x-sys-effect-glow-primary: #ABFF00; --x-sys-effect-glow-secondary: #FFB900; --x-sys-effect-shadow-primary: #033B00 (dark-green border companion to the lime glow); hover: #ABFF00; pressed: #568000 (olive).

Density & spacing Brand-Locked

--x-sys-space-s as base gap; rhythm: regular. Content is border-grouped — near-black panels with a visible #333540 hairline rather than pure whitespace grouping.

Elevation Brand-Locked

Cards/tiles: level-1-main on hover/active only (otherwise flat). Modals: level-2-main with full scrim. Toasts/alerts: level-3-main (nav alert badge sits above all other chrome).

Stroke weight Brand-Locked

--x-sys-stroke-thin (1px, observed #333540) for structural dividers/panel borders; --x-sys-stroke-medium for active/selected borders (observed 1px #ABFF00 top/bottom hairline on the "green" status state).

Material & texture Brand-Locked

Ambient starfield/particle noise texture (~15% opacity) behind event tiles; carbon-fibre texture behind the mobile nav flyout. No halftone, no distress/torn edges observed.

Motion language Brand-Locked

200ms ease-in-out for hover/active transforms; 300ms ease-in-out for opacity/accordion reveals; 500ms linear for nav show/hide slides. Feedback is glow-halo fade-in, not scale/bounce.

Iconography Brand-Locked

Solid-fill, geometric, sharp-cornered glyphs (chevrons, close/X, social icons) rendered as inline SVG data-URIs at small sizes — no illustrated or duotone icon style observed.

Semantic colour mapping Store-Flexible

Default: bg-action-primary → sys-colour-primary-main. Stores may map to any intensity step (subtle through inverse).

Interactive vs non-interactive rule

Interactive = elevation-level-1 + sys-colour-primary-main (acid-green) + glow-halo states overlay. Non-interactive = elevation-flat + sys-colour-neutral-subtle + no overlay, no glow.

08 · Art Direction & VFX

Imagery System

Framing & subject

¾-angle to full-body 3D character renders, always mid-action (never a passive T-pose or static portrait); camera roughly at chest height, subject placed off-centre to leave room for a title lockup.

Background

Dark, atmospheric, near-black environments — smoke, web strands, or abstract shadow silhouettes framing the edges; never a flat solid colour field behind hero art.

Lighting & mood

Single dramatic rim/key light per character, cool-to-neutral temperature, strong value contrast pushing the subject to read instantly against the dark backdrop.

VFX signature

Acid-green glow halo on featured/selected content; gold foil gradient + outline stroke on display type; soft smoke and web-strand silhouettes as edge framing devices.

Figure-ground

Separation via dramatic rim lighting and a dark vignette rather than a hard cutout — characters sit "in" the environment, not pasted on top of it.

Text-over-art

Titles sit directly over the key art (no dedicated safe-zone box) but always over the darkest region of the frame; large sizes get a 1px outline stroke to stay legible without a scrim.

✓ Do
  • Keep one dominant light source per character
  • Frame with smoke/shadow/web silhouettes at the edges, not the centre
  • Reserve the acid-green glow for the one featured/active element
✗ Don't
  • Flatten characters onto a plain solid-colour backdrop
  • Use more than one glow colour in the same frame
  • Drop hero type onto a bright region of the art without the outline stroke
