01 · Brand Essence

TACTICAL · AUTHENTIC
GRITTY · CINEMATIC · MISSION-BRIEFING

METAL GEAR SOLID Δ positions itself as a faithful, next-gen-fidelity remake — "The Beginning of it All." The site's own copy stresses technical credibility (pore-level facial detail, permanent battle scars) as the emotional hook, framed throughout in a restrained, dossier-like register.

Tactical [observed]

Nav labels (FOX HUNT, SCREENSHOTS), mission-report style news ("2,000,000 COPIES SOLD *As of 2/17/2026, per internal tracking"), a live server-status board for the multiplayer mode.

Authentic [observed]

"The same gripping story and engrossing world, now with all new graphics and 3D audio" — the copy leads with fidelity-to-original, not novelty.

Gritty [observed]

Battle-damage system copy: "the wear and tear of Snake's clothes, as well as bruises and bullet wounds on his body... Injuries will leave their mark on Snake's body permanently."

Cinematic [observed]

Hero key art: shallow depth of field, volumetric jungle fog, handheld-camera-adjacent framing on Snake's bandana-and-scar close-up.

Mission-briefing voice [observed]

Character bios read like dossier entries: "A GRU colonel known in the West by the codename 'Thunderbolt.' A sadist with a penchant for torture..." — factual, third-person, unembellished.

01·B · Brand Voice & Tone observed · live site copy

How MGSΔ Speaks

Measured, third-person, mission-briefing register. Marketing copy stays confident without ever tipping into hype; character bios read like intelligence-dossier entries.

  • White wordmark + green Δ on black or dark photographic backgrounds only (the only colourway observed)
  • Keep the Δ in its exact green (#009600) — never recolour it
✗ Misuse — never do this
  • Don't recolour the white wordmark or the green Δ
  • Don't separate "SNAKE EATER" from the main lockup

  • Don't place on busy light backgrounds without a dark scrim

Contrast & placement rules

  • White (#FFFFFF) on Void Black (#080808) ≈ 20:1 — the default pairing everywhere.
  • Field Green (#009600) on Void Black ≈ 5.1:1 — safe for body copy, unlike many saturated brand greens.
  • Pale Lime (#C4E39C) on Void Black ≈ 14.1:1 — safe for body copy.
  • White on the tertiary green CTA fill (#007E00) ≈ 3.9:1 — borderline; only acceptable at the observed bold/large button sizes (17–22px bold), never for small text.
  • PS5 blue and Xbox green are platform-badge colours only — never promote them to brand accents.
04 · Token Architecture — 5-Tier Cascade

How the design system is structured

Every visual property resolves through this cascade. Tiers 1–3 are brand-locked. Tiers 4–5 are store-flexible defaults. The emitter.ts only injects ref seeds + optional palette ramps + font + page background — everything else resolves automatically.

BRAND-LOCKED

Anchor rule: --x-palette-{role}-500 = var(--x-ref-{role})
The -500 step IS the ref seed.
BRAND-LOCKED
3

07 · Component Style DNA

Visual Building Blocks

Shape language Brand-Locked

--x-sys-radius-0 throughout — sharp corners everywhere. The one exception is a diagonal-cut bevel on CTA button corners (Tier-2 signature surface), not a rounded radius.

Lighting model Brand-Locked

Interactive buttons: solid green fill, bold condensed label, subtle bevel. Non-interactive chrome: flat near-black, no gradient. Key art: single directional light source through jungle fog.

Effect tints Brand-Locked

--x-sys-effect-shadow-primary: #003D00 · --x-sys-effect-glow-primary: #009600 · hover: brightens toward #1CB71C [inferred] · pressed: darkens to #007E00.

Density & spacing Brand-Locked

Regular rhythm, base gap maps to --x-sys-space-m. Space-grouped sections separated by whitespace, not borders.

Elevation Brand-Locked

--x-sys-elevation-flat everywhere — content sits directly on black or full-bleed photography.

Stroke weight Brand-Locked

A 5px solid green accent bar precedes every section title (.cmn-section-title .inner:before) — a decorative marker, not a structural divider.

Material & texture Brand-Locked

Clean flat UI chrome — texture lives entirely in the 3D-rendered imagery (battle damage, jungle fog), never as a UI overlay.

Motion language Brand-Locked

Scroll-triggered fade-ins, ~300ms ease-out [inferred]. No bouncy/snappy feedback observed.

Iconography Brand-Locked

Minimal — platform logos (PS5/Xbox/Steam) and a traffic-light status dot are the only icon-like elements observed.

Semantic colour mapping Store-Flexible

Default: bg-action-primary → sys-colour-primary-main. Stores may remap to any intensity step.

Interactive vs non-interactive rule

Interactive controls carry a solid green fill (or accent-bar marker) with a bold condensed uppercase label; everything else is flat near-black or full-bleed image with zero elevation.

08 · Art Direction & VFX

Imagery System

Framing & subject

Bust/¾ portraits for character cards; wide environmental framing with the subject often off-centre for key art.

Background

Environmental — jungle canopy, foliage, Cold-War military hardware — never solid or abstract.

Lighting & mood

Single directional key light through fog/foliage; cool-to-neutral temperature with occasional warm ember accents (Fox Hunt).

VFX signature

Volumetric fog, embers/sparks, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain.

Figure-ground

Environmental depth — characters composited into the rendered scene, not isolated cut-outs.

Text-on-art rule

Copy sits on the darkest region of the frame with a soft vignette darken, no hard scrim box.

✓ Do
  • Use fog/foliage to carry compositional weight
  • Reserve saturated green for a single focal accent
  • Keep lighting directional and motivated
✗ Don't
  • Don't add lens flares or cartoon-bright highlights — off-brand
  • Don't flatten characters onto solid-colour cut-outs
  • Don't saturate the whole frame in green
