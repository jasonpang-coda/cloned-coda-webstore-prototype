01 · Brand Essence

GRIMDARK · IMPERIAL · ORNATE · BAROQUE · COMMANDING

Rogue Trader's identity is one of gilded authority over an unforgiving void. Painterly grimdark realism meets baroque Imperial ornament: near-black backgrounds, gold filigree frames, inscriptional titling, and the cold green light of the warp. Everything reads weighty, ceremonial and commanding — the storefront of a dynasty, not a kiosk.

02 · Logo & Clear Space

Logo

"ROGUE TRADER" set in a metallic gold inscriptional Roman titling face (Stevens Titling Pro; webstore proxy Cinzel), seated on a steel sunburst/aquila medallion, with "WARHAMMER 40,000" in a condensed sans lockup above. The mark is gold-on-steel over void black.

✓ Approved use
  • Gold/steel mark on void-black or darkened scrim.
  • Maintain clear space ≥ the height of the central medallion on all sides.
  • Minimum legible width ~32mm print / 180px screen.
  • Place over an isolation panel when on busy key-art.
✗ Misuse — never
  • Recolour, flatten, or tint the gold/steel mark.
  • Place directly on high-detail painterly art without a scrim.
  • Stretch, rotate, or rearrange the WH40K / Rogue Trader lockup.
  • Add drop shadows or outer glows to the mark.

Contrast & placement rules

How the system is structured

Every visual property resolves through this cascade. Tiers 1–3 are brand-locked; tiers 4–5 are store-flexible defaults. The theme file injects only ref seeds + font + page background — everything else resolves automatically. In this repo the live vars drop the --x- prefix (use --ref-*, --sys-*, --bg-*…).

05 · Layout & Grid System System Constant

07 · Component Style DNA

Visual Building Blocks

Shape language Brand-Locked

Soft-but-squared: controls/inputs/thumbnails --sys-radius-xs; containers --sys-radius-s. Angular, militaristic — not pill-shaped. Motifs: gold hairline frames, corner fittings, aquila emblem.

Lighting model Brand-Locked

Interactive = elevation-level-1 + gold hairline + warm rim/sheen on hover. Non-interactive = flat + subtle fill + no shadow.

Effect tints Brand-Locked

shadow-primary #0c0a0a · shadow-secondary #6d5d3f · glow-primary #a1e967 (warp) · glow-secondary #e5c994 (gold). hover #e5c994 · pressed #548031.

Density & spacing Brand-Locked

Regular rhythm on a 4px base; base gap --sys-space-s. Border-grouped (gold hairlines separate content rather than whitespace alone).

Elevation Brand-Locked

Cards level-1-main; modals level-2-main; toasts level-3-main; non-interactive flat. Layers like inlaid panels, not floating cards.

Stroke weight Brand-Locked

Gold hairline --sys-stroke-thin for dividers/frames; medium for active borders; thick for emphasis.

Material & texture Brand-Locked

Gilded filigree frames + corner fittings; subtle parchment grain & ~4% noise overlay; metallic wear on gold only. No halftone.

Motion language Brand-Locked

200ms cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1); weighty & ceremonial. Gilded sheen sweep on hover; slow fade-up reveals; scale 0.98 on press. Never bouncy.

Iconography Brand-Locked

Solid/engraved, medium stroke, sharp corners; Imperial heraldic motifs (aquila, skull, cog) where apt.

Semantic colour mapping Store-Flexible

Default bg-action-primary → sys-colour-primary-main. Borders default to gold (secondary-soft). Any intensity step is overridable.

Interactive vs non-interactive rule

Interactive = elevation-level-1 + gold hairline border + sys-colour-*-main + states overlay. Non-interactive = elevation-flat + sys-colour-*-subtle + no overlay.

↑ Gilded filigree frame — the signature container treatment (gold hairline + inset)
08 · Art Direction & VFX

Imagery System

Framing & subject

Ensemble hero: a Rogue Trader leading a retinue, ¾ action angle, eye-level; subject placement rule-of-thirds.

Background

Environmental gothic void-ship / cathedral depth; desaturated, atmospheric haze.

Lighting & mood

Cool ambient with warm key/rim on heroes; high chiaroscuro; foreboding.

VFX signature

Warp-green energy orbs & lightning, gilded edge-light, embers, lens haze.

Figure-ground

Heroes rim-lit forward of a darker environment; scrim behind any overlaid type.

Text-over-art

Reserve a darker third for the gold logo/type; min body 14px; never on busy detail.

✓ Do
  • Keep characters warm against cool environments.
  • Use warp-green glow sparingly as a focal accent.
  • Frame SKU art on transparent/void with a top-right key light.
✗ Don't
  • Flatten to bright saturated fields (breaks grimdark).
  • Let type touch un-scrimmed painterly detail.
  • Add modern neon or clean flat-vector treatments.
