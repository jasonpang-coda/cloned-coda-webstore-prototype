01·B · Brand Voice & Tone observed · live site

How Master Duel Speaks

Read from the official Konami site's live copy. The voice is grand, welcoming, and event-driven — it treats the reader as a “Duelist” on a heroic path, speaks in the second person, and carries an energetic, exclamatory cadence without ever being edgy or aggressive. Heritage and authenticity are constant proof points.

Five voice pillars

1 · Grand & aspirational
Frames play as an epic ascent. Live copy: “Are you ready to enter the ultimate battle?”, “Become the top Duelist in the world”, “Road to Worlds”, “the definitive edition”.
2 · Authentic & heritage-proud
Leans on 20+ years of legacy. “An authentic Yu-Gi-Oh! Digital card game”, “evolving for over 20 years”.
3 · Energetic & direct
Second person, short exclamatory lines. “Download Now!!”, “Master the Summoning methods as you play!”.
4 · Welcoming to all levels
Reassures newcomers while respecting veterans. “Master the basic rules of the game as you play”, tutorials, “for everyone to enjoy”.
5 · Community & event-driven
Always pointing to the next Festival, tournament, or campaign. “A lot of exciting events and tournaments”, “Take part in a variety of different Festivals!”.
Commerce voice
Value-forward and trustworthy, not pushy. Store copy: “great prices”, “better prices there than when purchasing in-game”.

Lexicon — say / avoid

✓ Brand vocabulary
  • Duelist (the player), Duel, Summon (Synchro / Xyz / Pendulum / Link), Deck.
  • Gems (currency), Festival, Solo Mode, World Championship.
  • Verbs: Summon · Duel · Master · Build · Climb · Dominate · Enter the battle.
✗ Off-brand
  • Generic “gamer / user / player-1” instead of Duelist.
  • Aggressive trash-talk, slang, or meme voice — stay grand, not edgy.
  • Over-discounting language (“cheap”, “blowout”). Prefer “great value”, “bonus Gems”.
  • Cold/technical checkout copy — keep the heroic, welcoming warmth through purchase.

Webstore copy examples

Hero: “Enter the Ultimate Battle.” / Sub: “Top up your Gems and build the deck that conquers Worlds.”
SKU CTA: “Claim Your Gems” · bonus badge: “+ Free Gems included”
Scarcity badge: “Limited-Time — ends in 33 days”
Empty/confirmation: “Your Gems are on their way, Duelist.”
02 · Logo & Clear Space

Logo & Lockup

The master lockup is the Yu-Gi-Oh! franchise wordmark stacked above the MASTER DUEL sub-brand. The wordmark is a metallic-gold, sharply beveled display treatment with a dark outline for separation on busy art. Observed assets: /yugioh/masterduel/images/logo.png, plus platform badges (PlayStation, Xbox, Steam, Switch, App Store, Google Play). observed

YU-GI-OH!
MASTER DUEL
Clear space = height of the “M” on all sides

Rules

  • Clear space: minimum margin equal to the cap-height of the “M” in MASTER on every side.
  • Minimum size: 120px wide on screen (keeps the bevel + outline legible).
  • Colourways: metallic gold on dark (primary), or flat gold #E3B23C, or mono white on dark. Never mono black on light without the outline.
  • Isolation on art: always place on a darkened zone or add a subtle radial scrim behind the lockup.
✓ Approved
  • Metallic gold on deep navy / obsidian.
  • Mono white on dark art with scrim.
  • Maintain the stacked wordmark + sub-brand ratio.
✗ Misuse — never
  • Stretch, skew, or re-condense the wordmark.
  • Recolour to non-brand hues or add rainbow gradients.
  • Place gold-on-light or over busy art without isolation.
  • Rearrange, rotate, or add extra drop shadows.

Contrast & placement rules

  • Parchment text #F5F7FF on Obsidian Navy #0E1430 ≈ 16.5:1 — passes AA/AAA body.
  • Master Gold #E3B23C on Obsidian Navy ≈ 9.2:1 — safe for headings, prices, and large text.
  • CTA text uses Obsidian Navy #0E1430 on Master Gold fill ≈ 9.2:1 — dark-on-gold, never white-on-gold (white-on-gold ≈ 1.8:1, fails).
  • Duel Blue #2E8BE6 on navy ≈ 5.1:1 (AA body OK); Arcane Purple #7A4DB3 on navy ≈ 3.4:1 — large text / accents only.
  • Status never by colour alone — positive pairs a ✓, caution a ⏱/clock, negative a ⚠ + text label.
  • Colour hierarchy: gold = highest-value/primary action; blue = informational; purple = decorative energy only. Never use two accents at equal weight in one CTA cluster.
  • On art or hero imagery, place text only over a scrim (navy at ≥55% opacity) to hold the ratios above.
04 · Token Architecture — 5-Tier Cascade

How the design system is structured

Every visual property resolves through this cascade. Tiers 1–3 are brand-locked. Tiers 4–5 are store-flexible defaults. The emitter.ts only injects ref seeds + optional palette ramps + font + page background — everything else resolves automatically. Figma token exports drop the --x- prefix; the repo adds it.

06 · Typography

Type System System Constant Font + Sizes Brand-Locked

Brand font: Yu Gothic (游ゴシック) — the typeface used on the official Konami Master Duel site observed. It is a modern Japanese gothic (sans-serif) with clean, humanist letterforms and excellent Latin + Kana + Kanji coverage — a single family drives both headings and body. Yu Gothic is a system font (Windows/macOS), not on Google Fonts, so the web proxy for this guide is Noto Sans JP (the real name stays first in every CSS stack). Weight note: Yu Gothic ships Light/Regular/Medium/Bold, so extra-bold (800) falls back to Bold (700) when Yu Gothic renders; the Noto Sans JP proxy supplies a true 900 where the render allows.

Tier 1 — Type Primitives (Mobile / Desktop)

07 · Component Style DNA

Visual Building Blocks

Shape language Brand-Locked

Sharp, decisive, card-edged. Controls & inputs → --x-sys-radius-xs; containers/cards → --x-sys-radius-s; thumbnails → --x-sys-radius-xs; badges → --x-sys-radius-full. Signature motif: 45° cut-corner / beveled panel frame (see Tier-2).

Lighting model Brand-Locked

Interactive = --x-sys-elevation-level-1-main + gold/blue glow + higher saturation. Non-interactive = --x-sys-elevation-flat, no glow. Global light reads as an energy glow emanating from the CTA/price, not a physical sun.

Effect tints Brand-Locked

--x-sys-effect-glow-primary: #E3B23C · --x-sys-effect-glow-secondary: #2E8BE6 · --x-sys-effect-shadow-neutral: #05070F. Hover overlay #FFFFFF (≈8%); pressed overlay #000000 (≈12%).

Density & spacing Brand-Locked

Regular-to-tight rhythm on an 8px base; base gap --x-sys-space-s. SKU grids are border-grouped (card edges); nav/labels space-grouped. Touch target ≥ --x-sys-size-control-m (44px floor).

Elevation Brand-Locked

SKU/bundle cards & buttons → level-1-main; checkout sheet/modal → level-2-main; snackbar/toast → level-3-main; static banners → flat. Cards read as stacked cards on a table.

Stroke weight Brand-Locked

Dividers → --x-sys-stroke-thin (navy tint). Selected/active SKU & focus → --x-sys-stroke-medium in Master Gold. Emphasis frames → --x-sys-stroke-thick.

Material & texture Brand-Locked

Mostly clean flat on deep navy. Permitted accents: subtle radial energy glow behind hero art and a faint hex/foil sheen on premium cards only. Avoid heavy grain, halftone, or distress — this brand reads polished, not gritty.

Motion language Brand-Locked

Snappy & premium: 180ms ease-out for hovers, 240ms for sheets. Signatures: gold glow-pulse on primary CTA hover, scale-on-press (0.97), summon-style fade+rise on card reveal.

Iconography Brand-Locked

Solid / semi-illustrated, medium stroke, slightly sharp corners. The Gem icon is the hero token (faceted crystal). Status icons: ✓ / ⏱ / ⚠ paired with colour, never colour alone.

Semantic colour mapping Store-Flexible

Default: --x-bg-action-primary → --x-sys-colour-primary-main. Stores may remap to any intensity step (subtle → inverse) without touching brand tiers.

Interactive vs non-interactive rule

Interactive = elevation-level-1 + sys-colour-*-main/gold glow + states overlay. Non-interactive = elevation-flat + sys-colour-*-subtle + no glow + no overlay.

08 · Art Direction & VFX

Imagery System

Framing & subject

Hero: iconic monsters / signature cards at ¾ dynamic angle, mid-summon. SKU: the faceted Gem hoard, centred, denomination overlaid, camera slightly above.

Background

Deep obsidian-navy void with a radial energy bloom; optional duel-field grid perspective receding to a vanishing point.

Lighting & mood

Cool overall temperature, warm gold key light on the focal subject; strong rim light to separate from the void; high-contrast, dramatic.

VFX signature

Gold/blue energy particles, card-summon light shafts, arcane glyph rings, holographic foil glints, soft motion blur on cast energy.

Figure-ground

Subject cut out on the void with rim-light + drop glow; never busy environmental clutter behind product art.

Text-over-art

Reserve the lower third or one clear corner; add a navy radial scrim (≥55%). Min body 12px; prices in Master Gold, large.

✓ Do
  • Isolate one hero subject against the navy void with gold rim-light.
  • Let energy VFX radiate from the Gem / card, reinforcing value.
  • Keep denomination numerals huge and legible on SKU art.
✗ Don't
  • Crowd the frame with multiple competing subjects.
  • Use warm/muddy backgrounds that kill the cool-void contrast.
  • Place white type directly on gold or on unscrimmed art.
