01 · Brand Essence

Arcane · Competitive
Legendary

A card game brand that treats every duel as ceremony: 20+ years of competitive history, rendered through Egyptian-mythic gold seals, arcane violet energy, and cel-shaded anime action. Nothing about it is casual — it is ornate, declarative, and always addresses the player as a Duelist entering an arena, not a customer opening an app.

ARCANE

[observed] Gold hieroglyph-covered magic-circle seals recur across every hero banner and event thumbnail as the dominant background motif.

COMPETITIVE

[observed] Copy leads with "Become the top Duelist in the world" and "Duelist Cup" — competitive ranking is a headline feature, not a footnote.

LEGENDARY

[observed] "The definitive edition of the competitive card game that has been evolving for over 20 years!" — the brand sells pedigree, not novelty.

ORNATE

[observed] The wordmark itself carries chrome specular rendering and a red brush-ribbon mark — even the logotype is treated as a jewelled object.

DEFINITIVE

[observed] "Master the basic rules" / "Master the Summoning methods" — mastery framing repeats across tutorial and event copy alike.

01·B · Brand Voice & Tone observed · live site copy

How Master Duel Speaks

Grand and declarative — the brand talks like an arena announcer, not a storefront. It calls the reader "Duelist," leans on exclamation points and Title-Cased event names, and frames value through authority and reassurance rather than urgency or discounting.

Voice pillars

Definitive authority

"The definitive edition of the competitive card game that has been evolving for over 20 years!"

Challenge & stakes

"Are you ready to enter the ultimate battle?"

Mastery framing

"Master the basic rules of the game as you play!"

Abundance / scale

"Enjoy various Decks and Duels with over 10,000 different cards!"

Competitive aspiration

"Become the top Duelist in the world"

Value reassurance (commerce)

"You'll find better prices there than when purchasing items in-game."

Lexicon — say / avoid

✓ Brand vocabulary
  • Duelist (never "player" or "user")
  • Duel, Deck, Summon (Synchro / Xyz / Pendulum / Link)
  • Festival (event naming pattern)
  • "the ultimate battle" / "definitive edition"
✗ Off-brand
  • Casual internet slang
  • Discount-first framing ("cheap!", "sale!!!")
  • Generic "player" instead of "Duelist"
  • Hedging or passive phrasing

Webstore copy examples

Hero: "Enter the ultimate battle. Duel smarter with better value."
SKU CTA: "Master the Deck"
Bonus badge: "Duelist Bonus"
Scarcity badge: "Festival-Limited"
Confirmation: "Your Gems are linked to your KONAMI ID, Duelist."

02 · Logo & Clear Space

Logo

[observed] A two-part lockup: a small red brush-ribbon "Yu-Gi-Oh!" mark sits above the dominant element — a bold, italic-leaning, angular chrome/silver "MASTER DUEL" wordmark with sharp cut-corner strokes and a thin blue rim-glow, anchored by a downward-pointing triangle beneath it. Anniversary variants add a gold serif "N-TH ANNIVERSARY" line under the triangle — this is a dateable sub-lockup, not the base logo.

Reference asset reproduced at reduced resolution for internal design-reference use only. © Konami Digital Entertainment.

✓ Approved colourways
  • Chrome/silver wordmark on near-black or dark nebula background (as observed)
  • Red ribbon mark always kept in its native red — never recoloured
  • Full lockup (ribbon + wordmark + triangle) kept together, never split apart
✗ Misuse — never do this
  • Don't re-typeset "MASTER DUEL" in a webfont — it is bespoke cut logotype art
  • Don't place the chrome wordmark on a light/white background — it was built for dark grounds and loses all specular contrast
  • Don't tint the chrome silver to match a promo colour — it must stay neutral-metallic
  • Don't stretch or skew the lockup to fit a banner aspect ratio

Contrast & placement rules

  • Gold on neutral near-black ≈ 12.2:1 — safe for body text and headings alike
  • White (#F5F5F7) on neutral near-black ≈ 18.1:1 — default reversed body-text pairing
  • Silver/chrome (#E4E6EC) on deep navy panel (#12142A) ≈ 14.5:1 — matches the wordmark's own chrome-on-dark contrast
  • Secondary red on near-black ≈ 4.5:1 — meets the AA large-text floor only; reserve for headings/badges, never small body copy
  • Never rely on colour alone for status — positive pairs with a check icon, caution with a warning triangle, negative with an X or label
04 · Token Architecture — 5-Tier Cascade

How the design system is structured

Every visual property resolves through this cascade. Tiers 1–3 are brand-locked. Tiers 4–5 are store-flexible defaults. The emitter.ts only injects ref seeds + optional palette ramps + font + page background — everything else resolves automatically. Figma token exports drop the --x- prefix; the repo adds it.

BRAND-LOCKED

05 · Layout & Grid System System Constant

Non-Negotiable Layout

Identical in every guide. The brand never changes the grid. Responsiveness is driven by CSS @container queries against the device-screen container — never viewport @media. The screen sets container-type: inline-size; the overlay deliberately does not (it would trap position: fixed descendants).

07 · Component Style DNA

Visual Building Blocks

Shape language Brand-Locked

--x-sys-radius-0 (sharp) for containers/thumbnails, --x-sys-radius-full (pill) for controls and badges — mirrors the template's own pill-shaped buttons (border-radius: 10em/20em/50em observed) against otherwise sharp-cut card edges. Recurring motifs: downward-pointing triangle beneath the wordmark, circular hieroglyph-ring seals.

Lighting model Brand-Locked

Interactive: elevation-level-1 + higher saturation + rim-glow overlay (echoes the template's own #55f/#88f button hover glow). Non-interactive: elevation-flat + subtle, no shadow — background art stays flat/painterly, contrast comes from value not glow.

Effect tints Brand-Locked

--x-sys-effect-shadow-primary: #8A6410; --x-sys-effect-glow-primary: #F5DFA0; --x-sys-effect-glow-secondary (violet): #5B4FC7; hover: #7A6FDD; pressed: #4A3FA0.

Density & spacing Brand-Locked

Base gap: --x-sys-space-s. Rhythm: regular. Grouping: border-grouped — cards read like duel-field zones with clear edges, not loose whitespace clusters.

Elevation Brand-Locked

Cards: elevation-level-1-main. Modals: elevation-level-2-main (a "summoning ritual" — focused, everything else recedes). Toasts: level-3-main. Backgrounds: flat.

Stroke weight Brand-Locked

--x-sys-stroke-thin for dividers, --x-sys-stroke-medium for active/emphasis borders (gold or violet), --x-sys-stroke-thick reserved for premium/ritual framing only.

Material & texture Brand-Locked

Fine particle-sparkle screen-blend noise (~8%) drifting over dark backgrounds, not uniform grain. Gold hieroglyph-ring seal as a recurring watermark motif. No halftone, no distress — clean cel-shaded rendering throughout.

Motion language Brand-Locked

220ms ease-out. Glow-pulse on hover, particle drift/parallax on hero backgrounds, chevron-wipe transitions echoing the logo's triangle. Avoid bouncy/cartoon easing — the brand reads competitive and precise, not playful.

Iconography Brand-Locked

[inferred: high] Solid/duotone icon style with sharp corners, matching the sharp-cut logotype strokes; card-suit and Summon-method glyphs (Fusion/Synchro/Xyz/Pendulum/Link) as a natural icon-set extension.

Semantic colour mapping Store-Flexible

Default: bg-action-primary → sys-colour-primary-main (gold). Stores may map to any intensity step (subtle through inverse).

Interactive vs non-interactive rule

Interactive = elevation-level-1 + sys-colour-*-main + rim-glow states overlay, echoing the site's own hover-glow convention; non-interactive = elevation-flat + sys-colour-*-subtle + no overlay, letting the painterly background art carry the atmosphere instead.

08 · Art Direction & VFX

Imagery System

Framing & subject

3/4 or low-angle action pose, subject placed right-of-centre with negative space reserved left for logo/CTA overlay (observed consistently across hero banners).

Background

Near-black starfield/nebula with a dominant gold hieroglyph-ring seal and violet-blue arcane energy swirl behind the subject.

Lighting & mood

Single dramatic backlight source (the magic circle itself), cool violet-blue fill, warm gold rim — high value contrast pushes the character forward.

VFX signature

Chromatic bloom/lens flare, floating particle sparks, energy-trail streaks radiating from the light source.

Figure-ground

Cel-shaded character cut sharply against a soft-focus painterly background — no scrim needed, the value contrast alone separates subject from ground.

Text-over-art

Logo/headline zone is always the darker left/lower third; minimum comfortable type size ~24px on the darkest part of the gradient.

✓ Do
  • Reserve a dark, low-detail zone for logo/CTA placement
  • Keep one dominant light source per composition
  • Let the gold ring seal recur as a background signature, not a foreground gimmick
✗ Don't
  • Don't crowd the frame with multiple competing light sources
  • Don't place logo/type directly over the brightest part of the magic circle
  • Don't flatten the cel-shaded character style into painterly/photoreal rendering
