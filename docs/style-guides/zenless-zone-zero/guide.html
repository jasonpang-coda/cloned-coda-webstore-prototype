Style Guide · v1.0 · 2026

ZENLESS ZONE ZERO

A hyper-saturated urban-noir action game that treats its own patch notes like TV episodes and its UI chrome like a corporate operations terminal.

Source: https://zenless.hoyoverse.com/en-us/main · © COGNOSPHERE / HoYoverse — reference-derived fan style guide, not an official brand document.

01 · Brand Essence

KINETIC · DEADPAN-COOL · NEON-MAXIMAL

Zenless Zone Zero pairs a hyper-saturated, high-contrast visual language (pure black chrome, one screaming neon accent) with a surprisingly measured, almost bureaucratic voice — exact UTC timestamps and "Special Program" announcements sit right next to bold cel-shaded key art. The brand spans two visual registers depending on story arc: grounded urban-noir (homepage hero) and surreal cosmic (version-event splash pages) — both wrapped in the same black-and-neon chrome.

Kinetic [observed]

Hero key art freezes a mid-action moment (two operatives perched on a moving car) rather than a static pose; scroll-linked background transitions between sections confirm the whole homepage is built around motion, not a static poster.

Deadpan-cool [observed]

Copy stays measured even at its most exciting: "The Zenless Zone Zero Version 3.2 'Their Secret Histories' Special Program will begin on August 28 at 19:30 (UTC+8)!" — one exclamation mark, exact time zone, no hype stacking.

Neon-maximal chrome, minimal palette [observed: CSS]

Nav/header is pure black (#000000); the single CTA fill is an unmissable neon yellow-green (#D8FA00, confirmed in the live stylesheet on .nav-content-download). No competing accent shares that saturation.

Dual-world storytelling [observed]

The homepage hero is grounded urban-noir (parked car, industrial alley); the "Version 3.1 The Long Goodbye" splash sub-page is cosmic/ethereal (starfield, angel-winged figure) — same brand, two registers keyed to story arc.

How Zenless Zone Zero Speaks

Speaks like an in-world ops broadcast, not a marketing page — exact timestamps, named "episodes" for each update, and rarity-tier lexicon (S-Rank, W-Engine) doing double duty as both game terms and sales pitch.

02 · Logo & Clear Space

Logo

A bold, slightly-skewed 3D/extruded white block wordmark reading "Z·Z·Z" (three stacked/interlocking Z-cube forms), used at white-on-black exclusively in the observed chrome. No icon-plus-wordmark composite was observed — the mark is the wordmark.

Reference asset reproduced at reduced resolution for internal design-reference use only. © COGNOSPHERE / HoYoverse.

✓ Approved colourways
  • White wordmark on solid black (only colourway directly observed)
  • Clear space ≥ one wordmark-height on all sides
✗ Misuse — never do this
  • Don't recolour the wordmark into the neon accent — it stays neutral so the accent stays reserved for the CTA
  • Don't place on busy key art without a solid dark field behind it
  • Don't stretch or skew beyond its native extrusion angle

Contrast & placement rules

  • Black text on #D8FA00 neon ≈ 15.6:1 — clears AA/AAA for any size.
  • White wordmark on #000000 ≈ 21:1 — maximum contrast.
  • Body canvas on the live site is a light #EFEFEF grey, not pure white — this guide's dark-canonical treatment (#000000 neutral) is the brand's own chrome colour; use #EFEFEF only where a light-mode page background is specifically needed.
  • Statuses derived above (positive/caution) are flagged as inferred — verify against real error/success UI before shipping.
04 · Token Architecture — 5-Tier Cascade

How the design system is structured

Every visual property resolves through this cascade. Tiers 1–3 are brand-locked. Tiers 4–5 are store-flexible defaults. The emitter.ts only injects ref seeds + optional palette ramps + font + page background — everything else resolves automatically. Figma token exports drop the --x- prefix; the repo adds it.

1
Type primitives + Ref seeds
--x-sys-font-weight-{extra-bold|bold|semibold|regular}
--x-text-size-{h1..h7|body-l|body-main|body-s|body-xs}
--x-ref-{primary|secondary|tertiary|neutral|status-positive|status-caution|status-negative}
BRAND-LOCKED
2
Palette ramps
--x-palette-{role}-{50..950} · neutral adds -0 / -1000
Anchor rule: --x-palette-{role}-500 = var(--x-ref-{role})
The -500 step IS the ref seed. The ramp is built around it.
BRAND-LOCKED
3
System — colour intensity, dimensions, effects, states
--x-sys-colour-{role}-{subtle|soft|main|strong|heavy|inverse}
--x-sys-effect-{shadow|glow}-{role} · --x-states-{hover|pressed}
--x-sys-space-* · --x-sys-radius-* · --x-sys-stroke-{thin|medium|thick}
--x-sys-elevation-{flat|level-1|level-2|level-3}-{main|inverse} · --x-sys-blur
--x-shadow-shadow-* · --x-shadow-glow-*
BRAND-LOCKED
4
Semantic / component — store-overridable defaults
--x-bg-* · --x-text-* · --x-border-*

Per-store overrides: body[data-theme='theme-preset--{id}'] (specificity 0,1,1) beats the static mixin (0,1,0) — no !important needed.

05 · Layout & Grid System System Constant

Non-Negotiable Layout

Identical in every guide. The brand never changes the grid. Responsiveness is driven by CSS @container queries against the device-screen container — never viewport @media. The screen sets container-type: inline-size; the overlay deliberately does not (it would trap position: fixed descendants).

Visual Building Blocks

Shape language Brand-Locked

--x-sys-radius-full for controls/badges (67.5px pill, [observed: CSS]); --x-sys-radius-l inferred for containers. Signature motif: diagonal comic-panel-cut section dividers (see tier2SignatureSurfaces).

Lighting model Brand-Locked

Interactive: elevation-level-1 + higher-saturation neon step. Non-interactive: elevation-flat + subtle step. [inferred beyond the one observed CTA — card-level lighting was scroll-blocked]

Effect tints Brand-Locked

--x-sys-effect-glow-primary: #D8FA00; --x-sys-effect-glow-secondary: #29D4FF; hover: #C6E800; pressed: #A8C400 [hover/pressed inferred]

Density & spacing Brand-Locked

--x-sys-space-s as base gap; regular rhythm — neither cramped nor especially airy in the observed nav/hero chrome.

Elevation Brand-Locked

Cards: level-1-main [inferred]; modals: level-2-main [inferred]; non-interactive: flat.

Stroke weight Brand-Locked

--x-sys-stroke-thin for dividers; --x-sys-stroke-medium for active borders [inferred — no divider/border was directly observed].

Material & texture Brand-Locked

Clean flat — no halftone or grain observed. One signature motif: large diagonal panel-cut section transitions (see Art Direction).

Motion language Brand-Locked

Scroll-linked WebGL background transitions confirmed present (network/DOM evidence) though not renderable in this pass; assume 150ms ease-out for discrete UI feedback [inferred].

Iconography Brand-Locked

Platform/rating badges observed as flat solid icons on black pill fields — solid style, sharp corners on the icon marks themselves, pill on the container.

Semantic colour mapping Store-Flexible

Default: bg-action-primary → sys-colour-primary-main. Stores may map to any intensity step (subtle through inverse).

Interactive vs non-interactive rule

Interactive = elevation-level-1 + sys-colour-primary-main (the neon fill) + states overlay; non-interactive = elevation-flat + neutral black/white + no overlay.

08 · Art Direction & VFX

Imagery System

Framing & subject

Full-body or ¾ figures in dynamic mid-action poses (perched, leaning), not static standing portraits; camera at a slight low angle looking up at the subjects on the homepage hero.

Background

Environmental on the homepage (industrial alley/rooftop with signage and props); abstract starfield/cosmic void on version-event splash pages.

Lighting & mood

Cool blue-grey ambient light on the homepage scene with warm practical-light accents (signage glow); soft directional glow around the cosmic splash figure.

VFX signature

In-key-art light glow/rim treatment on subjects; no separate UI-chrome glow effect was directly observed.

Figure-ground

Full environmental depth on the hero (not a cut-out); ESRB badge and platform-icon row sit on solid black pill fields directly beneath the art, giving that one zone flat figure-ground separation.

Text-over-art

Large display CTA ("Download Now") is composited as a rotated sticker-style graphic directly on the key art, not a plain text overlay — a bespoke signature treatment, not a reusable scrim rule.

✓ Do
  • Keep exactly one neon-saturated accent per composition
  • Frame subjects mid-action, not static
  • Let environmental depth carry the background — avoid flattening key art onto a plain colour field
✗ Don't
  • Don't add halftone/grain texture — this brand is clean and flat outside its key art
  • Don't introduce a second high-saturation accent competing with the neon CTA
  • Don't reuse the cosmic/starfield register for standard commerce surfaces — it's reserved for major story-arc splash moments
