# Release Notes — v0.104.1

**Date:** September 2026
**Milestone:** Landscape device-frame bug fixes — corner clipping, checkout banner squash, Buy Now centering

---

## Highlights

- **Landscape corner-clipping fixed for good.** v0.104.0's `overlayInsetLeft` (a notch-specific fix) is replaced with `landscapeCornerInset` — a general "stop hugging the edge" inset applied to the whole store (scrollable page content via padding, sheets/drawers via the overlay's own position), sized to the screen's own corner radius. Landscape's much shorter screen made the frame's fixed corner radius eat into a much bigger share of the short dimension than it does in portrait; this keeps flush-edge content clear of that curve everywhere, not just around the notch.
- **The sticky bottom category nav is genuinely square and full-width in landscape**, without losing width or breaking the device frame's border. Since `landscapeCornerInset` already pushes `.device__overlay`'s left/right edges in by exactly the frame's own corner radius, its bottom corners can now safely square off (0 radius) in landscape only — that inset amount is precisely where a rounded rect's boundary becomes straight again, so the square corner lands exactly on the safe boundary instead of overflowing past the frame's curve. (A full-bleed-but-unclipped `.device__chrome` layer was tried and reverted in favor of this — no structural change needed, `CategoryNav` stays exactly where it was.)
- **Checkout sheet banner no longer squashes to a sliver in landscape.** `.sheet__banner`'s `overflow: hidden` made its flexbox automatic minimum size 0, so with landscape's much shorter screen leaving less room in the sheet's flex-column body, it was the one child flexbox was free to compress toward nothing instead of holding its 80px height and letting the body scroll (which it's already built to do). Fixed with `flex-shrink: 0`.
- **"Buy Now" button label re-centered.** COD:M's condensed heading font applies `scaleX(<1)` to the label, defaulting to a left-edge `transform-origin` (correct for left-aligned text) — the neighboring "View Terms" link already had the `transform-origin: center center` override for its own centered label; the Buy Now button never got the matching fix, so its label always drifted left of center (in any orientation, not landscape-specific).

## Changes

### `src/components/DeviceFrame.vue`

- `overlayInsetLeft` (v0.104.0, notch-specific) replaced with `landscapeCornerInset` (general corner clearance, `radius - bezel`, landscape only).
- `screenStyle` gains `paddingLeft`/`paddingRight: landscapeCornerInset` so scrolling page content clears the corner curve on both sides.
- `.device__overlay`'s `left`/`right` now both use `bezel + landscapeCornerInset` (was `left` only, sized to the notch).
- `.device__overlay`'s `borderRadius` squares off its bottom corners in landscape only (`Rpx Rpx 0 0`) — safe specifically because the corner inset above already provides the necessary clearance from the frame's curve.
- Transitions extended to cover `right`, `border-radius`, and the screen's `padding-left`/`padding-right`.

### `src/components/steps/CheckoutStepBody.vue`

- `.sheet__banner` gains `flex-shrink: 0`.

### `src/components/steps/CheckoutStepFooter.vue`

- `.sheet__checkout :deep(.btn__label)` gains `transform-origin: center center`, mirroring the existing `.sheet__terms-link` rule.

## Reverted this session (no longer present)

- A `.device__screen`/`.device__overlay` square-bottom-corners experiment (would have needed a much larger inset to stay clear of the frame's border, or accepted a visible border gap) — abandoned in favor of matching the frame's radius exactly, then later safely squaring only `.device__overlay`'s bottom corners once `landscapeCornerInset` made it geometrically safe to do so.
- An unclipped `.device__chrome` layer + `#chrome` slot for `CategoryNav`, moving it out of `.device__overlay` entirely — abandoned once the corner-inset math showed squaring the overlay's own bottom corners was sufficient, with no structural change needed.
