# Release Notes — v0.109.1

**Date:** September 2026
**Milestone:** Fixed the `pwa-install` dev tour's stale detour click (broken by v0.109.0's merged-sheet change) and added Android coverage

---

## Highlights

- **Fixed a real bug in the `pwa-install` tour guide**, introduced by v0.109.0's TaskGiftSheet merge: its final step used to open the sheet and click the footer CTA to "drill into" the iOS install-steps detour — but that detour no longer exists (instructions render inline now), so the click just closed the sheet immediately instead. The step also pointed at `task-gift-sheet-detour`, a `data-poi` that no longer exists in the DOM.
- **Added Android coverage** to the same tour: a narrated device-frame switch to Android, followed by reopening the drawer there and spotlighting TaskGiftSheet's Android-only variant (no checklist — just the single requirement as prose, and a "Turn on Notifications" CTA that requests permission directly).

## Changes

### `src/components/TaskGiftSheet.vue`

- Added two `data-poi` hooks that didn't exist before, needed for the tour to target them: `task-gift-sheet-instructions` (the inline media+steps block) and `task-gift-sheet-android-body` (the Android single-requirement paragraph).

### `src/tourguide/flows/pwa-install.tour.js`

- `ios-instructions` step: simplified to just open the sheet and spotlight the instructions block directly (no click needed any more).
- Three new steps: `switch-to-android` (narrated device-frame switch, no POI), `open-drawer-android` (reopens the drawer on the Android frame), `android-notification` (spotlights the Android variant's body copy, closes via the same deterministic composable call as the iOS step).
- `teardown` now also resets the device frame back to `'iphone'`, so a later tour run isn't left stuck on Android.
- Swept stale "detour"/"drills into" language out of the remaining comments.

## Verification

Ran the tour live in the browser through all 9 steps: confirmed the device frame visibly switches to Android (status bar icons/time format change), the sheet shows the correct Android-only content (no checklist, single CTA) at the new spotlight target, and teardown restores iPhone afterward. Build and preflight both clean.

## Known follow-up

- The `story-slide` step's `beforeStep` searches for `[data-poi="story-pwa-cta"]`, which no longer matches COD:M's current story-carousel install slide (it now routes through the gift-task flow instead) — the tour visibly stalls on that step during autoplay. Pre-existing, unrelated to this fix; flagged as a separate follow-up task rather than fixed here.
