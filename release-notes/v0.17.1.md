# Release Notes — v0.17.1

**Date:** June 2026
**Milestone:** FCM gift item images, success haptic global (gift claim + checkout)

---

## Highlights

- **FCM gift item images:** First Login Reward and Daily Gift bundle items now show real product art with quantities and probability tags wired from the catalog.
- **Global success haptic:** `triggerHaptic('success')` (`[10, 40, 20]`) fires on every success moment across all stores — gift claim confirmed, EA auto-confirm, and checkout CTA tap. Previously only sign-in fired this pattern.

---

## Composable changes

### `src/composables/useGiftClaim.js`

- **`confirmClaim()` haptic:** calls `triggerHaptic('success')` after adding the gift id to `claimedIds` and flipping `claimSuccess`.
- **EA auto-confirm haptic:** the 200ms setTimeout also calls `triggerHaptic('success')` when opening the sheet in success state post-sign-in.

---

## Component changes

### `src/components/CheckoutSheet.vue`

- **Checkout success haptic:** added `onCheckoutConfirm()` handler wired to `@click` on the checkout CTA. Fires `triggerHaptic('success')` on click (after the existing `v-haptic:confirm` pointerdown buzz). Applies to all stores — COD:M, FCM, eFootball.

---

## Store / catalog changes

### `src/stores/fcm/catalog.js`

- **Gift child image constants:** five new constants (`FCM_GIFT_FL_RANK_UP`, `FCM_GIFT_FL_GEM`, `FCM_GIFT_DG_PLAYER`, `FCM_GIFT_DG_GEM`, `FCM_GIFT_DG_COIN`) resolve webp paths from the `FCM_IMAGES` glob.
- **First Login Reward items:** `{ image: FCM_GIFT_FL_RANK_UP, quantity: 100 }`, `{ image: FCM_GIFT_FL_GEM, quantity: 3000 }` (was two null-image placeholders).
- **Daily Gift items:** four items with quantities and tags — Player Item `1` (tag `75-84`), Gem `100+` (tag `30%+`), Coin `10M` (tag `1%`), Coin `50K` (tag `100%`) (was three null-image placeholders).
