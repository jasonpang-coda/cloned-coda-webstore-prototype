# Release Notes — v0.20.1

**Date:** June 2026
**Milestone:** YGODL SKU card polish — selected-bg fix, title scale, rating badge scoped to COD:M, card corner-radius lift

---

## Highlights

- **YGODL selected card bg fix:** `--bg-card-selected` in the ygodl theme was resolving to `--sys-colour-primary-subtle` (= `primary-950` = `oklch(0.240 0.070 264)`) — near-invisible against the dark L1 surface. Overridden to `--sys-colour-primary-soft` (primary-700, medium electric blue) so the selected state is clearly perceptible.
- **SKU title scaled down for YGODL:** all five SKU card variants (`SkuCard`, `BundleSkuCard`, `GiftSkuCard`, `SkuImageCard`) switch from `text-style-heading-card` (H5, was 18px) to `text-style-heading-sku-title` (H7). YGODL keeps `--sys-size-h7: 14px`; COD:M, FCM, eFootball have `--sys-size-h7` bumped to 18px so their rendered size is unchanged. TDR was already at 18px.
- **Rating badge scoped to COD:M only:** `showRating` was `true` in TDR and YGODL. Both now set `showRating: false`, the `rating` asset import is removed, and `brand.rating` is `null`. COD:M remains the only store with the checkout footer rating badge.
- **SKU card corner-radius lift:** `SkuCard`, `BestSellerCard`, and `SkuImageCard` root radius bumped from `--radius-container-xs` (4px) to `--radius-container-s` (8px). `BundleSkuCard` and `GiftSkuCard` were already on container-s.
