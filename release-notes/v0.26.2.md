# Release Notes — v0.26.2

**Date:** June 2026
**Milestone:** Remove CP currency label from regular CP image SKU titles

---

## Highlights

- **Currency label removed from regular CP image SKUs** — the "CP" suffix no longer appears in the SKU title row of the final "Buy CP" image-card section (`#cat-cp-img`). The `:currency-label` prop is omitted from `SkuImageList`; `SkuImageCard.currencyLabel` defaults to `''` and the label span is gated by `v-if`, so no rendering change is needed at the component level.

---

## Component changes

### `src/App.vue`

- **Regular CP image section (`#cat-cp-img`):** removed `:currency-label="strings.currency.name"` from `<SkuImageList>`. The new-user discount section (`#cat-cp-img-newuser`) is unchanged and retains its currency label.
