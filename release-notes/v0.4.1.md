# CODM SKU Card 3.0 — v0.4.1

**Player Account section · checkout footer redesign · wider content column · bundle hero fade** · June 2026

Four design-driven updates building on the v0.4.0 Bundle overhaul.

---

## What's new

### `PlayerAccount.vue` — signed-out sign-in prompt
New section (Figma `5041:16027`) rendered right after the StoryCarousel, **only while signed out** (`v-if="!signedIn"` from `useAuth`); a signed-in variant will follow.

- **Sign in with COD:M** — outlined CTA (2px white ring, transparent fill): `codm-logomark` + label + `open_in_new` icon. Calls the shared `startSignIn()` 5s sim flow.
- **"⸺ Or ⸺"** separator.
- **Enter Your COD:M ID** — yellow underlined guest link, wired to a stub `onGuestSignIn()` with a TODO (guest form design pending).

All display text condensed; entrance via `slide-down` at `DELAY_ACCOUNT = 180` (cascade: story 100 → account 180 → bestseller 250). Added the `open_in_new` glyph to `MaterialIcon`.

### Checkout footer redesign (`CheckoutSheet.vue`)
Matches Figma `5135:20648`:
- Removed the "Tax will be added in next step" line.
- Added a **"Subtotal"** eyebrow above the price (12px label/regular, `--text-body-default`).
- Bumped the price **20px → 24px** (H2).

### Wider content column (`Span.vue`)
The `content`/`carousel` Span at M/L widens from **1/2 → 2/3**: `grid-column: 4/10 → 3/11` (8 of 12 cols), `max-width: 720px → 960px`. Both sizes share the rule by design, so SKU lists, hero, bundle, and carousels all gain width on tablet/desktop.

### Bundle SKU hero fade (`BundleSkuCard.vue`)
The 1:1 SKU hero composited on the banner now has a **bottom mask fade** (`linear-gradient(to bottom, #000 55%, transparent)`) so it dissolves into the breakdown row instead of ending on a hard square edge.
