# Release Notes — v0.60.7

**Date:** August 2026
**Milestone:** Layered sheet-footer motion + a Terms & Conditions legal bar on the payment sheet

---

## Highlights

- **Sticky footers move as their own layer.** Every bottom sheet with a
  sticky footer (`ItemSummarySheet`, `CheckoutSheet`, `OrderSummarySheet`,
  `ClaimGiftSheet`) now animates the footer separately from the panel: it
  settles a beat behind the panel on open, and detaches ahead of it on
  close — follow-through, using two new motion tokens.
- **Payment sheet gains a Terms & Conditions bar.** `CheckoutSheet` and
  `OrderSummarySheet` (the FCM Buy Now pilot's payment step) show a static
  legal disclaimer below the payment-channel list, plus a "View Terms and
  Conditions" link under the CTA that scrolls to it.
- **Terms-link cleanup.** `BuyNowBar`'s own "View Buy Now Terms &
  Conditions" link is removed — redundant now that the payment sheet
  carries the real Terms & Conditions copy.

## Changes

### `src/tokens/motion.css`

- New tokens: `--motion-sys-duration-footer-enter` (220ms),
  `--motion-sys-duration-footer-exit` (130ms),
  `--motion-sys-duration-footer-delay` (100ms), and the composite
  `--motion-sheet-footer-enter`/`--motion-sheet-footer-exit` (decelerate /
  accelerate respectively).

### `src/tokens/reduced-motion.css`

- `transition-delay` is now also zeroed under `prefers-reduced-motion`, so
  the footer's delayed enter collapses to instant instead of snapping after
  a lingering delay.

### `src/components/ItemSummarySheet.vue`, `CheckoutSheet.vue`, `OrderSummarySheet.vue`, `ClaimGiftSheet.vue`

- Added `.sheet__footer`-specific enter/leave transition rules layering the
  footer against the panel's existing slide (footer settles behind on
  enter, detaches ahead on exit).

### `src/components/CheckoutSheet.vue`, `OrderSummarySheet.vue`

- New static Terms & Conditions bar (`.sheet__terms`) rendered after the
  payment-channel list: heading in `.text-style-utility-default-bold`,
  body in `.text-style-utility-micro-regular` / `--text-body-default`.
- New "View Terms and Conditions" link (`.sheet__terms-link`,
  `.text-style-utility-default-regular`, `--text-hyperlink-default`) 16px
  below the CTA button that scrolls the legal bar into view.
- New `termsRef` + `onViewTerms()` wiring; footer CTA column restructured
  (`.sheet__checkout-cta` / `.sheet__footer-cta`) so the terms link gets
  its own 16px gap independent of the button/rating-badge spacing.

### `src/components/BuyNowBar.vue`

- Removed the "View Buy Now Terms & Conditions" link and its now-dead
  `.buynow__terms` CSS rule.

### `src/locale/commonStrings.js`

- New `common.checkout` keys: `termsHeading` ("Terms and Conditions"),
  `termsBody` (the EA license/T&C/final-sale disclaimer, referencing
  "Checkout" as the triggering action), `viewTerms` ("View Terms and
  Conditions").

## Verification

- `node_modules/.bin/vite build` passes.
- Verified live in the Browser pane preview on the FCM store: footer
  stagger timing confirmed via computed styles (100ms delay / 220ms
  decelerate on enter, 130ms accelerate / no delay on exit); Terms &
  Conditions bar renders statically (no accordion), correct copy and
  colour tokens; "View Terms and Conditions" link scrolls it into view.
