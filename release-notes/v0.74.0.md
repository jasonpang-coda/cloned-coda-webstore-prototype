# Release Notes — v0.74.0

**Date:** August 2026
**Milestone:** `material-fx` skill — new "tasteful UI translation" doctrine and a component-scale restraint demo

---

## Highlights

- **New ref file, `ui-translation.md`.** The skill teaches materials from
  real optics, but a physically-correct recipe (a full chrome mirror, a
  literal ±45° carbon twill) regularly reads as too literal once it's
  shrunk onto a 130px card next to eleven identical siblings — that gap had
  no general rule, just a one-off exception documented for the Codashop SKU
  card pilot (`codashop-mapping.md`). This generalizes it: keep the ONE
  identity cue that makes a material recognizable, drop the reinforcing
  cues that were pulling weight at a larger scale, and reserve the full
  treatment for hero/large surfaces. Includes a scale-aware keep/drop table
  for all 7 materials, four authoring gates (two already existed elsewhere
  in the skill — one-light-per-page, static-by-default; two are new —
  an FX budget, and "materials are semantic, not decorative"), a net-new
  semantic material→meaning map (glass=overlays, metal=premium,
  carbon=gaming, paper=receipts, etc.), and an epistemic note keeping its
  budget numbers (blur px, alpha/grain ceilings) clearly separated from
  `physics.md`'s measured-optics claims.
- **Correction to an early draft of this doctrine.** A first pass at this
  table (proposed by a teammate) listed carbon fibre's ±45° crosshatch +
  antiphase shimmer as the cue to *keep* — but that's exactly the photoreal
  cost the Codashop pilot *dropped* for reading as busy fabric under SKU
  price text. Fixed by making the table scale-aware: the literal weave is
  now explicitly reserved for hero/large surfaces, with the near-black
  base + one faint diagonal hint as the UI-scale identity cue — matching
  what actually shipped.
- **New standalone demo, `demo-components.html`.** Companion to the
  existing `demo.html` (a swatch lab — isolated material tokens, no real
  UI). This one renders real components — card, CTA button, badge, sheet
  header, price tag, input — across all 7 materials in one grid, each
  annotated with its kept/dropped cue. Centrepiece: a discrete
  Minimal/Balanced/Photoreal restraint control (not a continuous intensity
  dial — you can't uniformly scalar-scale blur-px, alpha, grain, and
  animate-or-not along one axis) that sets the real authoring budgets live,
  so "Photoreal" visibly over-budgets every material at once (full carbon
  weave, a travelling metal sweep) and starts competing with the labels it
  sits behind — the failure mode the doctrine exists to prevent. Unlike
  `demo.html`'s scene-light sliders (wired to `--scene-light-*` but never
  actually consumed by any recipe), this demo's azimuth/elevation controls
  are wired to `--x-light-gradient-angle` and every specimen's highlight
  angle genuinely follows them. Standalone, no build step,
  `prefers-reduced-motion` guarded.
- **`SKILL.md` wiring.** New step 6 in the order-of-operations ("down-
  translate for the surface"), a new §7 summarizing the doctrine, three new
  pre-ship checklist items, and the frontmatter `Refs:` list updated.
- **Cross-links.** `codashop-mapping.md`'s SKU-card-pilot section now
  points to `ui-translation.md` as the doctrine it was generalized into
  (and no longer calls itself a one-off, non-precedent-setting exception).
  `physics.md`'s central unverified-numbers list gained a pointer to
  `ui-translation.md`'s budgets, explicitly distinguishing them as
  authoring conventions, not optics claims.
