# Release Notes — v0.93.1

**Date:** September 2026
**Milestone:** Order Complete gift banner respects claimed state; design-harness story coverage extended to Order Complete and Transaction History pages

---

## Highlights

- **Order Complete gift banner hides once the 88 CP reward is claimed.** The task-gated gift banner (`OrderCompletePage.vue`) previously stayed visible in every step, including after claiming — degrading to a dead "Got it" pill while its title/description still read "Claim your 88 CP" and pushed PWA install/notification copy at a user who'd already done both. It now drops out entirely once `useTaskGiftClaim().step === 'claimed'`, matching the already-persisted `claimed` flag (localStorage-backed, shared with the Gifts-category card).
- **New harness stories:** `OrderCompletePage.stories.js` (install step, push step, and gift-already-claimed variants) and `TransactionHistoryPage.stories.js`, plus matching handoff specs under `docs/Handoff/components/order-complete-page/` and `docs/Handoff/components/transaction-history-page/`.

## Changes

### `src/components/OrderCompletePage.vue`

- Gift banner's `v-if` now also checks `giftTaskStep !== 'claimed'`, so the banner (which doubles as the page's PWA-install touchpoint) never shows once the reward is claimed.

## New files

- `src/library/stories/OrderCompletePage.stories.js`, `src/library/stories/TransactionHistoryPage.stories.js`
- `docs/Handoff/components/order-complete-page/spec.md`, `docs/Handoff/components/transaction-history-page/spec.md`
